Our storyFive years, five chapters. Here's how a two-person ops shop became the team companies call when they need something built, shipped and scaled.

2021
This is where it started. Two laptops, one spare room, very bad Wi-Fi.

Chapter 01 — 2021: The spare room

We started with the jobs nobody wanted.

In 2021, Ballark Labs was two people fixing broken spreadsheets, cleaning up CRMs and wiring tools together for small businesses. Unglamorous work. But it taught us how companies actually run — and where they quietly lose hours every day.

Our first real client was a pharmaceutical distributor drowning in order emails. Three people spent their mornings copying orders into an ERP by hand. We built a small automation that did it in seconds.

30 hours a week, given back. They told three friends. Those friends told theirs.

2022
This one surprised us too.

Chapter 02 — 2022: The 12-day translation

Nine weeks, they were told. We did it in twelve days.

That same distributor was expanding into six new markets. Their product leaflets and labelling had to be translated and pass regulatory review. The agency quote: nine weeks, no guarantees.

We built a terminology pipeline, paired it with specialist linguists, and ran every line through a second review. Twelve days later, all six languages passed review on the first submission.

Our translation practice was born. Today it covers 40+ languages, from pharma SmPCs to legal contracts.

2023
Suddenly every client had the same question.

Chapter 03 — 2023: AI shows up for work

“Can AI just do this?” Sometimes, yes.

When generative AI arrived, our inbox filled with one question. We stopped writing proposals and started building. Our first agent handled customer support for a regional logistics company — tracking queries, delivery changes, refunds — around the clock.

That same year, a client’s launch film fell through two weeks before go-live. No crew, no budget. We made it with AI animation in six days, and it became their most-watched video ever.

4,000 support tickets a month, resolved by one agent. And a brand-new film practice.

2024
This is the email that changed what we are.

Chapter 04 — 2024: From vendor to partner

“Could you just be our tech team?”

A healthcare client sent us that one line in March 2024. They didn’t want another vendor. They wanted someone who understood their business and could build whatever came next.

So we did. We built them a compliance tracking product for clinics, then turned it into vertical SaaS other clinics could license. And because great products still need customers, we added performance marketing to get them in front of the right people.

Our first SaaS product, now used by 60+ clinics. Our first retainer that read “outsourced technology partner.”

2025–26
And here we are. Bigger team, same habits.

Chapter 05 — 2025–2026: Scale smart

Five practices. One team that already knows your business.

Today, Ballark Labs is 48 engineers, linguists, animators and marketers working in small pods. Each pod stays with a client for the long run, so nobody has to explain their business twice.

We still move fast. We’ve just learned to build things that keep working when you grow 10x.

140+ clients, 11 countries, and a founder who still answers hello@ballarklabs.com.

That’s the story so far. The next chapter might be yours.

Questions we get a lot.

Do you only work with large enterprises?

No. We started with small businesses and still love them. Our clients range from 10-person startups to companies with thousands of employees.

Why one agency for five different services?

Because the work overlaps. A new product needs automation, translation, a launch film and paid growth. One team means one brief, one timeline and no finger-pointing between vendors.

How fast can you start?

Usually within a week of our first call. Small automations can go live in days; most MVPs ship in 6–10 weeks.

Will AI replace the humans on our project?

No. AI makes our team faster. Every agent, translation and film is reviewed by a specialist before it reaches you or your customers.

Is our data safe?

Yes. We sign NDAs, follow least-access principles, keep data in the region you choose and can deploy agents inside your own cloud.

Do we own what you build?

Yes. The code, content and IP are yours once the project is paid for.

What does it cost?

Projects start from a few thousand dollars; pod retainers are priced monthly. You’ll get a clear, fixed quote after our first call.
